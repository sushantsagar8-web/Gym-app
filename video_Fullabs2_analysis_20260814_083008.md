This video demonstrates a core exercise:

* **Primary Exercise:** Reverse Crunches
* **Target Abdominal Region:** Full abs
* **Repetitions or Timed Hold:** Repetitions (20)
* **Form/Safety Notes:** Lie flat on your back with arms by your sides for stability. Lift your legs and curl your hips toward your chest, then slowly lower them back down without letting your feet touch the floor to maintain tension.
